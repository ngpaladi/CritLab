How this works & caveats

For each second it computes the power you'd need to hold your measured speed alone (gravity from the elevation grade + rolling resistance + wind-aware aero + inertia), then divides your actual watts by it. Low ratio = someone's punching the hole for you. The absolute % shifts with CdA/wind assumptions (drag the sliders to see), but the pattern — which stretches and which matches were exposed — is robust. Live intervals.icu pull and auto-fetched historical wind need a backend; this page runs fully offline.
