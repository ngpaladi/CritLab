Shaded stretches are where your draft ratio sat at or above the clean air threshold. The lower band is the ratio itself, blue below 1.0 (someone else doing the work) and red above (you doing more than a solo rider would need).

Every lap, every sector the repeated-leak view

One row per lap, one column per stretch of circuit. A column that stays red top to bottom is a place you were working harder than usual every single lap — that is a positioning habit, not bad luck.

Unlike the map, this grid is scaled to your own median for this race rather than to an absolute 1.0, because its job is comparing stretches to each other. If you sat in the bunch all evening, every sector is sheltered in absolute terms and only the relative differences carry information.

What each corner costs

Exit cost is the work above CP spent getting back to entry speed after the apex. Bars are coloured by the draft ratio on the exit: a red bar means you were paying for that acceleration in clean air, with nobody to follow.
